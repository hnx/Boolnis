About

Boolnis is an independent, privately held organization that owns, holds, and oversees a portfolio of subsidiaries and other associated entities.

Its portfolio may comprise both legally registered and unregistered entities, operating in various capacities and across different fields.

An entity held by Boolnis may take various forms, including, but not limited to:

  • Companies and Organizations: Legally registered or unregistered business entities, organizations, and institutional structures.
  • Identities and Brands: Brands, trademarks, trade names, projects, and other distinct identities.
  • Assets and Holdings: Physical or digital assets, intellectual property, properties, and other forms of holdings.
  • Other Entities: Any other independent or subordinate structure, undertaking, or holding recognized and maintained within Boolnis’s organizational portfolio.

Boolnis is currently not legally registered as a formal business or organizational entity.
